FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions before your stay?

Here are some common questions travellers may have when planning accommodation in Rameshwaram.

Make An Enquiry →
How can I enquire about a room?

You can use the direct enquiry option on our website or contact Hotel Tamil Nadu by phone at 09657 282767. Share your travel dates and guest requirements so availability can be checked.

How far in advance should I enquire?

It is advisable to enquire as early as possible, particularly during pilgrimage seasons, holidays and other busy travel periods.

Can I request a particular room?

You can mention your preferred room option when making an enquiry. Requests are subject to availability and should be confirmed directly with the hotel.

Is direct booking available?

Yes. The website provides a direct enquiry option. Contact the hotel with your dates, guest count and requirements to check availability and booking details.

Can I contact the hotel by phone?

Yes. You can contact Hotel Tamil Nadu at 09657 282767 for accommodation enquiries and booking information.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Include your check-in and check-out dates, number of adults and children, preferred accommodation and any important requirements.
