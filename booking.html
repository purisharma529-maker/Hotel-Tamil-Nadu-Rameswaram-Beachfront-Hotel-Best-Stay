BOOKING FAQ

Frequently asked questions about booking.

Quick answers to common questions about making a direct stay enquiry.

How can I book a room at Hotel Tamil Nadu?

You can make a direct enquiry using the booking form on this page, call 09657 282767 or contact the hotel through WhatsApp. Your reservation is confirmed only after availability and booking details are confirmed by the hotel.

Can I make a direct booking enquiry?

Yes. The website is designed to make direct accommodation enquiries easy. Provide your dates, guest details and room preference and the hotel can respond to your request.

Can I book Hotel Tamil Nadu through WhatsApp?

You can contact the hotel through WhatsApp to discuss your stay and accommodation enquiry. Use the WhatsApp buttons on this page to start a conversation.

What information should I provide when booking?

Provide your name, phone number, expected check-in and check-out dates, number of adults and children and preferred room type. You can also add any special requirements.

Does submitting the form guarantee a room?

No. The form is an accommodation enquiry. A reservation is subject to availability and confirmation by the hotel.

Can I ask about room availability before travelling?

Yes. Providing your intended travel dates allows the hotel to review your accommodation enquiry and discuss availability with you.

Can I request a particular room type?

Yes. You can select your preferred accommodation type in the enquiry form. If you are unsure, select "Not sure" and explain your requirements in the message field.

Can I contact the hotel if I have questions before booking?

Yes. You can call 09657 282767, email info@hoteltamilnadu.com or contact the hotel through WhatsApp. You can also use the dedicated Contact page.
