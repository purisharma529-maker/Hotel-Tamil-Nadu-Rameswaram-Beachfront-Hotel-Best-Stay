Your base for discovering Rameshwaram

PLACES TO VISIT IN RAMESHWARAM

See the places that make Rameshwaram special

Whether you are visiting for pilgrimage, history, architecture or the coast, these are some of the places worth including in your Rameshwaram itinerary.

PILGRIMAGE · ARCHITECTURE

Ramanathaswamy Temple

The Ramanathaswamy Temple is the spiritual centre of Rameshwaram and one of the most important pilgrimage sites in India. Its long corridors, ornate architecture and sacred traditions make it a major reason travellers come to the island.

Temple guide →
SACRED SHORE · PILGRIMAGE

Agni Theertham

Located by the sea near the Ramanathaswamy Temple, Agni Theertham is an important sacred waterfront associated with the pilgrimage traditions of Rameshwaram.

About Agni Theertham →
ENGINEERING · LANDMARK

Pamban Bridge

The Pamban crossing is one of the defining landmarks of the journey to Rameshwaram. Surrounded by the waters of the Palk Strait, the bridge offers a memorable introduction to the island.

Discover Pamban Bridge →
COAST · HISTORY

Dhanushkodi

At the eastern end of the island, Dhanushkodi is known for its striking meeting of sea and sand, remnants of the old settlement and a landscape shaped by the devastating cyclone of 1964.

Visit Dhanushkodi →
HISTORY · MEMORIAL

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial

Rameshwaram was the birthplace of Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, India's former President and renowned aerospace scientist. The memorial in Pei Karumbu commemorates his life, work and remarkable journey.

Memorial guide →
TEMPLE · HERITAGE

Kothandaramaswamy Temple

Set in the quieter landscape towards Dhanushkodi, Kothandaramaswamy Temple is associated with the Ramayana traditions of the region and makes an interesting addition to a wider island itinerary.

Explore the temple →

Where is Hotel Tamil Nadu in Rameshwaram?

Hotel Tamil Nadu is in Rameshwaram, Tamil Nadu, making it a convenient base for visitors exploring the town, Ramanathaswamy Temple and other attractions around the island.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

The standard check-in time is 12:00 PM and the standard check-out time is 11:00 AM. If you have a special arrival or departure requirement, contact the hotel before your visit.

Can I contact the hotel directly?

Yes. You can contact Hotel Tamil Nadu directly by phone, email or WhatsApp to enquire about room availability, dates and stay requirements.

What places can I visit from Rameshwaram?

Popular places include Ramanathaswamy Temple, Agni Theertham, Pamban Bridge, Dhanushkodi, Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial and Kothandaramaswamy Temple. The island also offers beaches, coastal landscapes and smaller heritage sites to explore.

Is Rameshwaram suitable for a family trip?

Yes. Rameshwaram is visited by pilgrims, families, couples and leisure travellers. A family itinerary can combine temple visits with sightseeing around Pamban, Dhanushkodi and the coast.

How can I enquire about a room?

Use the Book Your Stay option on this website or contact the hotel directly on 09657 282767. You can also send your enquiry through WhatsApp.
