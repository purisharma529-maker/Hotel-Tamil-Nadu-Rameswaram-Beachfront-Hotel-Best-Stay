OUR STORY

Your stay is part of the journey.

Hotel Tamil Nadu is positioned as a practical accommodation choice for travellers who want a place to stay while experiencing Rameshwaram and its surrounding attractions.

Rather than treating the hotel as an isolated destination, this website is designed to help you plan the complete visit — where to stay, what to see and how to make a direct enquiry.

LOCATION

Stay in Rameshwaram and make the most of your visit.

Rameshwaram is located on Pamban Island in Tamil Nadu and is connected to mainland India through the Pamban area.

The town is an important base for visitors exploring the Ramanathaswamy Temple, Pamban Bridge, Dhanushkodi and other places around the island.

Why do people visit Rameshwaram?

Rameshwaram is an important pilgrimage destination, particularly because of the Ramanathaswamy Temple. Visitors also come to explore its coastal landscape, history and nearby places such as Pamban and Dhanushkodi.

Is Rameshwaram suitable for a family trip?

Yes. Families can combine temple visits with sightseeing around the island and nearby coastal attractions. The ideal itinerary depends on the time available and the interests of the group.

Can I enquire about accommodation directly?

Yes. You can use the hotel's direct enquiry page or call 09657 282767. Providing your travel dates and guest requirements helps the hotel respond more accurately.

What should I see during a Rameshwaram visit?

A typical itinerary may include the Ramanathaswamy Temple, Pamban Bridge area, Dhanushkodi and places connected with Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam. There are also other temples and coastal places worth exploring.
